# Tabletop decorative assets

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool for the selected B · Tabletop direction. These decorate the app and do not replace the shared role-art placeholder.

- [wolf-seal.png](wolf-seal.png): transparent wolf/moon seal used in screen headers.
- [village.png](village.png): transparent low-contrast panorama used in contextual banners.

The original generated PNGs are preserved. Decorative images have empty alt text; meaningful page titles remain real text. Every card continues to use the shared placeholder until its own role image is supplied.

## Prompt set

### tabletop-seal

Use case: illustration-story. Asset for a refined minimal medieval tabletop companion app. Create a single small round wolf-and-moon seal, square canvas, isolated on TRUE transparent background. A dignified dark forest-green silhouette of a wolf in profile howling upward, backed by a soft pale ivory full moon, within one very fine aged brass circular ring. Sophisticated ink and very restrained watercolor wash. Simple bold silhouette readable at just 48px. Almost no tiny detail, no fur texture clutter, no text, no letters, no words, no shield, no drop shadow outside the circle, no surrounding ornamental frame or stars. The whole seal fills 90 percent of the canvas; genuinely transparent corners. Main colors deep green #244838, ivory #F7F6F1, muted brass #B49762. A beautiful tabletop board-game maker's stamp, mature, quiet, not a cartoon mascot.

### tabletop-village

Use case: illustration-story. Very wide panoramic decorative header asset for a LIGHT minimal medieval tabletop companion app, approximately 5:1 horizontal aspect ratio. TRUE transparent background. Extremely delicate warm-gray ink and pale sepia watercolor silhouette of a small medieval village across a low horizon: a few simple rooftops and a bell tower at left, distant trees in the center, one old windmill at far right. Fine understated inkbook illustration, spare and atmospheric, large empty transparent sky above. Buildings and trees occupy lower third, not full image height. Very low contrast warm taupe #B9AC96 linework, most washes barely visible. No dark strokes, no black, no colored sky, no text, no UI, no emblem, no people, no frame, no paper texture or opaque background. Designed to sit behind a screen's title without competing with legible text. Keep composition calm and almost monochrome. Not photorealistic, not cartoon.
