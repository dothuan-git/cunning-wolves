# Art

- [card-back.webp](card-back.webp): the back of every role card. Square, 1024px; the card frame crops it with `object-cover`, so keep the design centered.
- [woodcut-village.webp](woodcut-village.webp): the home screen's village. It is a crop of the top of _The Werewolf_ by Lucas Cranach the Elder, about 1512, held by The Metropolitan Museum of Art, released as CC0 ([source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Werewolf_or_the_Cannibal_MET_DP842190.jpg)). Only the house, the castle and the villagers are used, because the lower half of the print is graphic. It is greyscaled, and its levels lift the scan's grey paper to white so it prints onto the page ground with `mix-blend-multiply`. Credited in the menu sheet (`credits.woodcut`).
- [card-art-fallback.webp](card-art-fallback.webp): the figure shown for any role without its own art, and when an image fails to load. 720px wide.
- [faction-village.webp](faction-village.webp), [faction-werewolf.webp](faction-werewolf.webp) and [faction-solo.webp](faction-solo.webp): the faction logos, used in `FactionEmblem`. 384px, shown at 192px.

Role artwork lives in [`../cards/`](../cards/README.md).
