# Angel / Thiên thần

- **id:** `angel`
- **Team:** solo
- **Wakes at night:** no
- **Marks:** none
- **Card art:** missing

## Rule (vi)

Trước đêm 1, làng bỏ phiếu loại một người. Nếu là mình, hoặc mình chết đêm 1, thắng ngay. Không thì thành dân làng thường.

## Rule (en)

Before Night 1, the village votes someone out. If it is you, or you die on Night 1, you win at once. Otherwise you become a plain villager.

## Narrator notes

Before Night 1, the village debates and votes someone out: hold that vote, then start the game and mark the death in Night 1. If the Angel was voted out, or dies on Night 1, they win and the game ends: press Finish. Otherwise the Angel plays as a plain villager.
