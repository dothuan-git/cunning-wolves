# Insomniac / Người mất ngủ

- **id:** `insomniac`
- **Team:** village
- **Wakes at night:** yes (500), after `troublemaker` and last
- **Card art:** `apps/web/src/assets/cards/insomniac.webp`

## Rule (vi)

Mỗi đêm, sau khi mọi người đã hành động xong, bạn biết ít nhất một trong hai người ngồi cạnh có thức dậy không, nhưng không biết là ai.

## Rule (en)

Each night, after everyone else has acted, you learn whether at least one of your two neighbors woke up. You do not learn which one.

## Narrator notes

From Ultimate Werewolf (Bézier Games). Wake them last, after everyone else has slept. Sources differ between editions: check the wording against your copy. The app shows the card and rule only; it does not run this role's power.
