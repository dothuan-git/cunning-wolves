# Troublemaker / Kẻ gây rối

- **id:** `troublemaker`
- **Team:** village
- **Wakes at night:** yes (490), after `raven` and before `insomniac`
- **Card art:** `apps/web/src/assets/cards/troublemaker.webp`

## Rule (vi)

Một lần trong ván, chọn một đêm: hôm sau làng loại hai người thay vì một.

## Rule (en)

Once per game, choose a night: the next day the village eliminates two players instead of one.

## Narrator notes

From Ultimate Werewolf (Bézier Games). If the vote ties, the chance is wasted. Sources differ between editions: check the wording against your copy. The app shows the card and rule only; it does not run this role's power.
