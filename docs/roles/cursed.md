# Cursed / Kẻ bị nguyền

- **id:** `cursed`
- **Team:** village
- **Wakes at night:** no
- **Changes roles:** yes: the narrator can give a player another role while it is dealt
- **Card art:** `apps/web/src/assets/cards/cursed.webp`

## Rule (vi)

Bạn là dân làng. Nếu sói tấn công, bạn không chết mà bí mật thành sói, và từ đó thức dậy cùng bầy sói.

## Rule (en)

You are a villager. If the werewolves attack you, you do not die: you secretly become a werewolf and wake with them from then on.

## Narrator notes

From Ultimate Werewolf (Bézier Games). The app shows the card and rule only; it does not run this role's power.
