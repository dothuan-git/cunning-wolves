# Baker / Thợ làm bánh

- **id:** `baker`
- **Team:** village
- **Wakes at night:** yes (400), after `chupacabra` and before `pyromaniac`
- **Marks:** none yet
- **Card art:** missing

## Rule (vi)

Mỗi đêm, khi bầy sói đi ngủ thì thức dậy, có một phần tư cơ hội nhìn thấy một con sói.

## Rule (en)

Each night, as the werewolves go back to sleep, you wake and have a one-in-four chance of seeing one of them.

## Narrator notes

From The Village expansion (the Bakery). The narrator rolls the chance (for example with a die or a card draw). The app shows the card and rule only; it does not run this role's power.
