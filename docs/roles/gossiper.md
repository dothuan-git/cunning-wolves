# Gossiper / Kẻ buôn chuyện

- **id:** `gossiper`
- **Team:** village
- **Wakes at night:** yes (60, Night 1 only), after `hoodlum` and before `the-count`
- **Card art:** `apps/web/src/assets/cards/gossiper.webp`

## Rule (vi)

Đêm 1: quản trò chỉ cho bạn một người giữ vai quan trọng nhất của mỗi phe (dân làng, sói, độc lập), theo thứ tự ngẫu nhiên. Bạn không biết ai thuộc phe nào.

## Rule (en)

Night 1: the narrator points at one player from each faction who holds that faction's most important role, in random order. You don't learn which is which.

## Narrator notes

Made for this app; it is not a role from the published games. Pick the strongest dealt role in each faction (for example the Seer, the Big Bad Wolf, or a solo role). Never pick the Gossiper themselves. Skip the solo faction when no solo role is in the game, so the Gossiper then sees two players. Shuffle the order before pointing. The role list is already public, so the power gives the Gossiper who holds the roles, not which roles exist. The app shows the card and rule only; it does not run this role's power.
