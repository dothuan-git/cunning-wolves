# Wild Child / Đứa trẻ hoang dã

- **id:** `wild-child`
- **Team:** village
- **Wakes at night:** yes (100, Night 1 only), after `three-brothers` and before `wolf-hound`
- **Marks:** `role-model` (on the chosen player), `turned` (on the Wild Child once the model dies)
- **Card art:** missing

## Rule (vi)

Đêm 1: chọn một người làm hình mẫu. Khi hình mẫu còn sống, bạn là dân làng. Khi họ chết, bạn thành ma sói và thức dậy cùng bầy sói từ đêm sau.

## Rule (en)

Night 1: choose a role model. While your model lives, you are a villager. When they die, you become a werewolf and wake with the pack from the next night.

## Narrator notes

Night 1: the Wild Child points at a role model; mark them "Role model". When the model dies, mark the Wild Child "Turned wolf": from the next night they wake with the wolves, and they appear in the wolves' panel. Not telling the village whether a dead Wild Child had turned keeps the doubt alive.
