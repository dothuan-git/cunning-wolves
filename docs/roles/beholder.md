# Beholder / Người chứng kiến

- **id:** `beholder`
- **Team:** village
- **Wakes at night:** yes (200, Night 1 only), after `seer` and before `aura-seer`
- **Card art:** `apps/web/src/assets/cards/beholder.webp`

## Rule (vi)

Đêm 1: quản trò cho bạn biết ai là Tiên tri. Sau đó bạn chỉ là dân làng thường.

## Rule (en)

Night 1: the narrator tells you who the Seer is. After that you are a plain villager.

## Narrator notes

From Ultimate Werewolf (Bézier Games). Wake them while the Seer is awake, and point at the Seer. The app shows the card and rule only; it does not run this role's power.
