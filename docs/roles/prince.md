# Prince / Hoàng tử

- **id:** `prince`
- **Team:** village
- **Balance:** +3 (official, Ultimate Werewolf: Ultimate Edition rulebook)
- **Wakes at night:** no
- **Card art:** `apps/web/src/assets/cards/prince.webp`

## Rule (vi)

Nếu bỏ phiếu sinh tử sắp treo cổ bạn, hãy lật bài: bạn sống sót, hôm đó không ai bị loại, và làng đi ngủ.

## Rule (en)

If the life-or-death vote would hang you, show your card: you survive, nobody is eliminated that day, and the village goes to sleep.

## Narrator notes

From Ultimate Werewolf (Bézier Games). The app shows the card and rule only; it does not run this role's power.
