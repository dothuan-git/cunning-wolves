# Bogeyman / Ông ba bị

- **id:** `bogeyman`
- **Team:** solo
- **Wakes at night:** yes (380), after `sorceress` and before `chupacabra`
- **Card art:** missing

## Rule (vi)

Mỗi đêm, nếu bầy sói không thống nhất được nạn nhân, bạn chọn thay chúng. Bạn chơi một mình và thắng khi mọi người có thức dậy ban đêm đều đã chết.

## Rule (en)

Each night, if the werewolves cannot agree on a victim, you choose one for them. You play alone and win when every player who wakes at night is dead.

## Narrator notes

From Ultimate Werewolf (Bézier Games). Listed as solo because it has its own win condition. Sources differ between editions: check the wording against your copy. The app shows the card and rule only; it does not run this role's power.
