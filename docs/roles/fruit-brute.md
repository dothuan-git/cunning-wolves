# Fruit Brute / Sói chán ăn

- **id:** `fruit-brute`
- **Team:** werewolf
- **Wakes at night:** yes (270), after `wolf-cub` and before `dire-wolf`
- **Card art:** `apps/web/src/assets/cards/fruit-brute.webp`

## Rule (vi)

Bạn thức dậy cùng bầy sói. Nếu bạn là con sói cuối cùng còn sống, bạn mất khẩu vị và không giết được nữa, nhưng vẫn thắng khi dân làng bị loại hết.

## Rule (en)

You wake with the werewolves. If you are the last werewolf alive, you lose your appetite and can no longer kill, but you still win once every villager is gone.

## Narrator notes

From Ultimate Werewolf (Bézier Games). Only one source gave this rule. Sources differ between editions: check the wording against your copy. The app shows the card and rule only; it does not run this role's power.
