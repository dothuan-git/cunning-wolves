## Rule (vi)

Mỗi đêm chọn một nạn nhân: họ chết vào ban ngày hôm sau. Sói không giết được bạn. Bạn thắng khi chỉ còn ma cà rồng.

## Rule (en)

Each night, choose a victim: they die the next day. Werewolves cannot kill you. You win when only vampires are left.

## Narrator notes

From Ultimate Werewolf (Bézier Games). Several players can be Vampires; they wake together. Sources differ between editions: check the wording against your copy. The app shows the card and rule only; it does not run this role's power.
