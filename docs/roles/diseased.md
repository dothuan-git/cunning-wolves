# Diseased / Người bệnh

- **id:** `diseased`
- **Team:** village
- **Wakes at night:** no
- **Card art:** `apps/web/src/assets/cards/diseased.webp`

## Rule (vi)

Nếu bị sói giết, đêm sau bầy sói bị ốm nên không giết được ai.

## Rule (en)

If the werewolves kill you, they are too sick to kill anyone the next night.

## Narrator notes

From Ultimate Werewolf (Bézier Games). Sources differ between editions: check the wording against your copy. The app shows the card and rule only; it does not run this role's power.
