# Confessor / Cha giải tội

- **id:** `confessor`
- **Team:** village
- **Balance:** +2 (estimate, compared with the Beholder)
- **Wakes at night:** yes (215), after `aura-seer` and before `bodyguard`
- **Card art:** `apps/web/src/assets/cards/confessor.webp`

## Rule (vi)

Một lần trong ván, vào ban đêm, chọn một người: họ cho bạn xem lá vai của mình. Bạn không được nói về vai của mình hay vai của họ cho đến khi bạn lên giàn treo cổ.

## Rule (en)

Once per game, at night, choose a player: they show you their role card. You can't talk about your role or theirs until you go on the gallows.

## Narrator notes

From The Village expansion (the Church), changed for a private role card. Wake the Confessor each night until they use the power. They point at a player and you show them that player's card; the player stays asleep. The gag lifts only when the hanging vote puts the Confessor on the gallows (see the day vote in `docs/game-rules.md`): let them speak about their role and the player they saw, even if the life-or-death vote spares them. If the wolves kill them at night, they stay silent. The app shows the card and rule only; it does not run this role's power.
