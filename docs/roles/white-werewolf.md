# White Werewolf / Sói trắng

- **id:** `white-werewolf`
- **Team:** solo
- **Wakes at night:** yes (360, with the wolves every night, and alone on even nights), after `mighty-wolf-mother` and before `sorceress`
- **Marks:** none
- **Card art:** missing

## Rule (vi)

Mỗi đêm thức dậy cùng bầy sói. Cách một đêm, bạn thức dậy một mình và có thể giết một ma sói. Bạn chỉ thắng khi là người sống sót cuối cùng.

## Rule (en)

Wake with the werewolves each night. Every other night, wake alone and you may kill one werewolf. You win only as the last player alive.

## Narrator notes

Wakes with the wolves every night (the app lists them in the wolves' panel too). On even nights, wake them alone after the wolves: they may point at a werewolf, who dies. They win only as the last player alive.
