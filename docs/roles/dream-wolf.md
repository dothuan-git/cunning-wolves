# Dream Wolf / Sói mộng

- **id:** `dream-wolf`
- **Team:** werewolf
- **Wakes at night:** yes (310), after `minion` and before `big-bad-wolf`
- **Changes roles:** yes: the narrator can give a player another role while it is dealt
- **Card art:** `apps/web/src/assets/cards/dream-wolf.webp`

## Rule (vi)

Bạn thuộc phe sói nhưng không thức dậy cùng bầy sói. Khi một con sói chết, bạn thế chỗ nó và từ đó thức dậy cùng bầy sói.

## Rule (en)

You are on the werewolf team but do not wake with the pack. When a werewolf dies, you take their place and wake with the pack from then on.

## Narrator notes

From Ultimate Werewolf (Bézier Games). Start waking them with the werewolves once one has died. Sources differ between editions: check the wording against your copy. The app shows the card and rule only; it does not run this role's power.
