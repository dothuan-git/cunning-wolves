# Devoted Servant / Người hầu trung thành

- **id:** `devoted-servant`
- **Team:** village
- **Wakes at night:** no
- **Changes roles:** yes: the narrator can give a player another role while it is dealt
- **Card art:** `apps/web/src/assets/cards/devoted-servant.webp`

## Rule (vi)

Khi làng loại một người, trước khi lộ vai của họ, bạn có thể lộ diện và nhận vai đó. Bạn chơi vai mới như thể đó là đêm đầu tiên. Không dùng được nếu bạn đang trong cặp đôi.

## Rule (en)

When the village votes someone out, before their role is revealed, you may reveal yourself and take their role. You play it as if it were the first night. Not allowed if you are a lover.

## Narrator notes

Wait before showing the voted-out card, so the Servant can speak up. The app does not change anyone's role: if the Servant swaps, tell them their new role in private and call it from the next night. The app shows the card and rule only; it does not run this role's power.
