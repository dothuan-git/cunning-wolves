# Bonesetter / Thầy nắn xương

- **id:** `bonesetter`
- **Team:** village
- **Balance:** +2 (estimate)
- **Wakes at night:** yes (495), after `troublemaker` and before `insomniac`
- **Card art:** `apps/web/src/assets/cards/bonesetter.webp`

## Rule (vi)

Một lần trong ván, vào ban đêm, chọn một người khác: họ được lấy lại năng lực đã dùng.

## Rule (en)

Once per game, at night, choose another player: they get back a power they have already used.

## Narrator notes

From The Village expansion (the Hovel), changed to a night power (the official Bonesetter acts at any time of day). Wake the Bonesetter each night until they use the power. The app shows the card and rule only; it does not run this role's power.
