# Three Brothers / Ba anh em

- **id:** `three-brothers`
- **Team:** village
- **Wakes at night:** yes (30), after `two-sisters` and before `fox`
- **Card art:** missing

## Rule (vi)

Ba anh em cùng thức dậy vào ban đêm, nhận ra nhau và có thể lặng lẽ trao đổi nghi ngờ.

## Rule (en)

The brothers wake together at night, recognize each other and may silently share their suspicions.

## Narrator notes

Pick three copies. Wake them on Night 1 so they meet; on later nights wake them only if the group wants them to talk.
