# Wolf-hound / Chó lai sói

- **id:** `wolf-hound`
- **Team:** village
- **Wakes at night:** yes (110, Night 1 only), after `wild-child` and before `actor`
- **Marks:** `chose-wolves` (on the Wolf-hound, if they chose the wolves)
- **Card art:** missing

## Rule (vi)

Đêm 1: chọn một lần duy nhất: dân làng hay sói. Chọn sói: thức dậy cùng bầy sói mỗi đêm. Không thì vẫn là dân làng.

## Rule (en)

Night 1: choose once and for all, villager or werewolf. As a werewolf, you wake with the pack every night. Otherwise you stay a villager.

## Narrator notes

Night 1: the Wolf-hound chooses by gesture, once and for all. If they choose the wolves, mark them "Chose the wolves": they wake with the wolves from then on and appear in the wolves' panel. Otherwise they sleep and play as a villager.
