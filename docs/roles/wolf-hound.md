# Wolf-hound / Chó lai sói

- **id:** `wolf-hound`
- **Team:** village
- **Wakes at night:** yes (110, Night 1 only), after `wild-child` and before `actor`
- **Changes roles:** yes: the narrator can give a player another role while it is dealt
- **Card art:** missing

## Rule (vi)

Đêm 1: chọn một lần duy nhất làm dân làng hay làm sói. Nếu chọn sói, bạn thức dậy cùng bầy sói mỗi đêm và cùng chọn nạn nhân. Nếu không, bạn ngủ tiếp và thắng cùng dân làng.

## Rule (en)

Night 1: choose, once and for all, to be a villager or a werewolf. A werewolf wakes with the pack every night and helps choose the victim. A villager stays asleep and wins with the village.

## Narrator notes

Night 1: the Wolf-hound chooses by gesture, once and for all. If they choose the wolves, change their role to Werewolf: they wake with the wolves from then on, in the wolves' panel. Otherwise they sleep and play as a villager.
