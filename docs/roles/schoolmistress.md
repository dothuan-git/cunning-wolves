# Schoolmistress / Cô giáo

- **id:** `schoolmistress`
- **Team:** village
- **Balance:** +1 (estimate)
- **Wakes at night:** yes (485), after `raven` and before `troublemaker`
- **Card art:** `apps/web/src/assets/cards/schoolmistress.webp`

## Rule (vi)

Một lần trong ván, vào ban đêm, chọn hai người: họ chỉ biết khi lần bỏ phiếu treo cổ hôm sau bắt đầu, và không được bỏ phiếu lần đó. Họ vẫn được nói.

## Rule (en)

Once per game, at night, choose two players: they don't learn it until the next hanging vote starts, and then they can't vote in it. They may still talk.

## Narrator notes

From The Village expansion (the School), changed for a private role card: the official Schoolmistress forbids two players openly each day and never votes. Wake the Schoolmistress each night until they use the power. When the hanging vote starts, tell the two chosen players they cannot vote in it. The app shows the card and rule only; it does not run this role's power.
