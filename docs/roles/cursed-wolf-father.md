# Cursed Wolf-Father / Sói nguyền

- **id:** `cursed-wolf-father`
- **Team:** werewolf
- **Balance:** −8 (estimate, compared with the Wolf Cub)
- **Wakes at night:** yes (330), after `big-bad-wolf` and before `alpha-wolf`
- **Changes roles:** yes: the narrator can give a player another role while it is dealt
- **Card art:** `apps/web/src/assets/cards/cursed-wolf-father.webp`

## Rule (vi)

Mỗi đêm bạn thức dậy cùng bầy sói và cùng chọn một nạn nhân. Một lần trong ván, sau khi bầy sói ngủ, bạn giơ tay: nạn nhân không chết mà bị nguyền, bí mật thành sói. Họ vẫn giữ năng lực ban đêm của mình.

## Rule (en)

You wake with the werewolves each night and pick a victim together. Once per game, after the pack goes back to sleep, raise your hand: the victim does not die but is cursed and secretly becomes a werewolf. They keep their own night power too.

## Narrator notes

After the wolves sleep, ask whether the Cursed Wolf-Father raises a hand. If so, the victim does not die: touch them and change their role to Werewolf, and they wake with the wolves from the next night (and still wake for their own power). The power is used once. Cursing the Bear Tamer makes the bear growl every morning.
