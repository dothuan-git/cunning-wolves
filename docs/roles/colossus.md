# Colossus / Người khổng lồ

- **id:** `colossus`
- **Team:** village
- **Wakes at night:** no
- **Card art:** `apps/web/src/assets/cards/colossus.webp`

## Rule (vi)

Nếu bị sói giết ban đêm, bạn kéo theo một con sói xuống mồ: quản trò chỉ ra các con sói và bạn chọn một con để loại.

## Rule (en)

If the werewolves kill you at night, you take one werewolf with you: the narrator points out the werewolves and you choose one to eliminate.

## Narrator notes

From the 25th anniversary edition of Millers Hollow (Asmodee France’s character presentation). Village side. When the werewolves kill the Colossus at night, point out the werewolves to them and eliminate the one they choose. The app shows the card and rule only; it does not run this role’s power.
