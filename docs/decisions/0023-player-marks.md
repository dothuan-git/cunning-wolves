# 0023 — Player marks, a third faction and wake nights

## Context

The narrator page only knew Alive and Dead, and a role was either village or werewolf. Thuan wants every role of the _Werewolves of Millers Hollow_ family, including third-faction and team-changing ones (Cupid, Accursed Wolf-Father, Wild Child, Pied Piper, White Wolf…). Each of those leaves something on a player that the narrator must remember: a title, a used potion, an infection, a lover. Some roles also wake only on Night 1 or every other night.

## Choice

- **Marks.** A mark is pool data in `packages/shared/src/roles/marks.ts` (`MARKS`): an id, a `{ vi, en }` label, the roles whose presence offers it (`from`; empty means every game), whether only those roles' holders can carry it (`holdersOnly`), and whether only one player carries it at a time (`single`: marking another player moves it). `marksFor(roleId, dealt)` says which marks a player may carry.
- The room keeps `GameState.marks` (player id → mark id → the phase it was marked in), stored in its own `marks` table so older rooms load with none. One master-only action, `toggleMark { playerId, mark }`, puts a mark on in the current phase or takes it off. It is refused for a mark the game doesn't offer that player. Marks reach the master view only, and seated players after Finish, like deaths.
- **This change ships flag marks for the current pool:** Sheriff (every game, single), Second vote used (Stuttering Judge), Power lost (Fox, Big Bad Wolf), Heal and Kill potion used (Witch), Extra life used (Elder), Can't vote (Village Idiot), and Rusted (Knight, on any player).
- **Narrator page:** Dead stays a one-tap pill. Beside it, a tag button with the mark count opens a sheet of the marks this player may carry, as switches. Carried marks show as chips under the name. The summary shows them on the Roles tab and in the journal phase they were made in.
- **Third faction:** `Team` gains `solo`, for roles that win on their own. Its hue is purple (`solo` / `solo-wash`), taken from the ribbon of its emblem, a half-masked hood Thuan generated (`faction-solo.webp`). Setup, the rules review and the summary list it after the wolves and the village, and a custom role can belong to it.
- **Wake nights:** each pool role has `wakeNights`: `every`, `first` (Night 1 only) or `even`. On a night a role doesn't wake, the narrator page lists it with the roles that never wake. The Stuttering Judge is `first`.
- A player's own phone never changes because of a mark; the narrator tells them in person.

## Why

- One kind of mark (an on/off flag, recorded with its phase) covers every role in the family with one mechanism, instead of special code per role. Adding a role's marks is data.
- **The third-faction roles use the same flag marks, with no link between players.** Lovers are two players each carrying "Lover", and the Wild Child's model is one player carrying "Role model" (a single mark). The narrator sees who is linked from the chips; the app keeps no pointer from one player to another.
- **The wolves' pack.** A mark can have `joinsPack` (Cursed, Turned wolf, Chose the wolves), and `PACK_ROLES` names roles that wake with the wolves without being wolves (the White Werewolf). Such players are listed in the first wolf role's panel on the narrator page, with their own role beside the name, so the narrator wakes everyone who feeds. Their own panel and notes stay. The app does not change anyone's role or team.
- **Nine more pool roles** use these marks: Cupid, Wild Child, Wolf-hound, Cursed Wolf-Father, White Werewolf, Pied Piper, Angel, and Prejudiced Manipulator (also sold as the Abominable Sectarian). The wake order was renumbered (10 … 160) to place them. A Town Crier mark was added beside Sheriff.
- **The rest of the pool.** Thuan asked to see every role's card and rule first, so the pool also holds Thief, Actor, Devoted Servant, Gypsy, Pyromaniac and the Village expansion's Farmer, Bartender, Barber, Bailiff, Confessor, Baker, Schoolmistress, Bonesetter, Lord and Vagabond: 42 roles in all, with no marks and no handling of their powers yet (the narrator does these by hand). The Thief needs two extra cards, which the card-count rule (roles = players) does not allow yet.
- **Ultimate Werewolf roles.** Thuan also asked for the roles of Ultimate Werewolf that the pool lacked: 33 more, again as a card and a rule only. Bézier Games' own role list could not be read, so the rules were written from fan wikis and a RulesPal rulebook summary; where they disagree, the role's doc says to check it against the owner's copy. The Vampire, Cult Leader, Tanner, Bogeyman, Lone Wolf, Chupacabra and Hoodlum are `solo`; the Minion, Sorceress, Wolf Man, Wolf Cub, Dire Wolf, Dream Wolf, Fruit Brute and Mighty Wolf-Mother are on the wolf team; the narrator page shows the wolves' pack under the plain Werewolf.
- **Roles with no sourced rule.** Thuan asked to add them anyway, using the most common version of the rule, since the narrator can edit a rule per room: Fruit Brute, Lone Wolf, Chupacabra, The Count, Hoodlum and the Amulet of Protection. The four 2026 anniversary roles (Puppeteer, Wise Monkey, Colossus, Mighty Wolf-Mother) have only a flavor description online, so their rules are placeholders written from it, and their docs say to replace them with the box text. The Teenage Werewolf is not added: no source describes it. The pool holds 86 roles.
- Keeping Dead as its own pill keeps the most frequent action at one tap in a dark room; a dropdown would hide it and could hold only one status.
- The app still never resolves abilities: a mark is a record, and nothing happens automatically when one is made.
- Roles that swap cards (Thief, Actor, Devoted Servant) need extra cards in the deck and a different deal. They are in the pool as a card and a rule only; the app does not run their powers.
- Mason and Villager-Villager were removed (same as Two Sisters and Villager); the Alpha Wolf now kills one more player on even nights, per Thuan.
